Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about regulatory approvals, timelines, payment schedules, and customizations.

What is the exact legal RERA status and registration number?

The Verdant Estate is fully approved and registered under Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority with RERA Registration Number: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/220324/004812. All sanction plans, title deeds, and NOCs are publicly available and can be inspected at our sales gallery or on the official K-RERA portal.

What is the scheduled possession date for the project?

Construction is proceeding at an advanced phase in strict accordance with the master schedule. Phase 1 possession (Towers A & B) is scheduled for June 2026, and Phase 2 (Towers C & D) along with the Clubhouse will be handed over by December 2026.

Which major banks and financial institutions have approved the project?

The Verdant Estate has received pre-approved loan sanctions from all premier nationalized and private banks including State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank, offering attractive home loan interest rates and flexible payment schemes.

What are the key sustainability and green building credentials?

The project is pre-certified IGBC Platinum rated. It integrates zero-discharge sewage treatment (STP), 100% rainwater harvesting, rooftop solar photovoltaic panels for common lighting, EV charging provisions for all parking bays, and 78% native botanical greenery.

Can I customize the interior layout or smart home specifications?

Yes. Homebuyers booking during the current construction phase can consult with our dedicated in-house architectural team to select bespoke flooring options, smart home automation packages, and modular kitchen upgrades.
